Questions

What is OTC VIP monitoring?

Ongoing monitoring of an OTC desk's counterparty book. You tell BlockQuery which accounts and prospects to watch. We track them continuously across 30+ blockchains and send a written report weekly or monthly on what they did everywhere else: volume, which venues and desks they routed through, and what changed.

Is this a data feed or a dashboard?

Neither. It is a monitored service delivered as a written report. There is nothing to integrate, no dashboard to learn and no licence to negotiate. If you want the underlying attribution as a feed to run yourself, that is a separate product: entity data feeds.

What do we need to provide?

A list of the counterparties and prospects to monitor. No systems access and no integration. The coverage list is yours, and you can change it.

How is it priced?

A fixed monthly fee, not priced per account or per query. Pricing is shared on request.

How does BlockQuery know who is behind an address?

Through the Chainlabs attribution layer: 18,000 commercial entities, including 800+ OTC desks and 480+ regional exchanges, built over six years. Every attribution is human-verified by a team of intelligence analysts and carries verifiable source evidence. How entity attribution works.

Does it predict price or generate trading signals?

No. BlockQuery sells data and monitoring, not signals. The report tells you what your counterparties did on-chain and where. What you do with that is your call.
